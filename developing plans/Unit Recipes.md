# Unit Recipes

Every army unit is made at the Commander's Table from **two kinds of item**. Fill in each
unit's two items and how many of each. They can go in either cost slot. Use the item's
in-game name (for example "Bone", "Gold Ingot", "Faction Coin").

These are the recipes in the mod now. Change any of them here and tell Claude to update the mod.
