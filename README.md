# The Crown of Chaos

In the vast and ever-changing world of Minecraft, whispers of a new and unimaginable power have begun to spread. Legends speak of the ancient Crown of Chaos, a dark artifact imbued with unimaginable strength. To craft this crown, however, a hero must first face the fiercest challenges the land has ever known.

A Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.2 by Warfist that adds powerful crowns. Craft a Golden
Crown, then upgrade it into one of the magical crowns. Each is worn in the helmet slot,
protects like a diamond helmet, and grants its own effects and abilities for as long as it
is worn. Every magical crown can also be upgraded to netherite.

> **AI disclosure:** The code for this mod was written with the help of AI (Claude, by
> Anthropic). All of the art (item sprites, the logo and the 3D crown models) was drawn by
> hand by the mod's creators.

## Crowns

Every magical crown shimmers with the enchanted glint (the Golden Crown only once enchanted). All
crowns except the Golden Crown have a diamond helmet's armor, toughness, durability and enchantability
and are repaired with diamonds. Effects last only while the crown is on your head; potion and beacon
effects you already have are left untouched. **R** is the crown ability key and **G** the secondary
one (both rebindable, see **Abilities**).

### Netherite crowns

Every crown except the Golden Crown can be upgraded at a smithing table, just like a diamond
helmet: a **Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template**, the crown, and a **Netherite Ingot**. The
result is "Netherite" plus the crown's name (for example, Netherite Crown of Sun). It keeps
the crown's enchantments, effects, abilities and look, and gets netherite helmet stats: more
armor and toughness, knockback resistance, more durability, and it doesn't burn in fire or
lava. The recipes unlock when you pick up a Netherite Ingot.

- Primary abilities with a cooldown show it on the crown's icon, and pressing the key early shows the time

  (Wind Charge, Wither Skull) show the charges left after every use instead.

  don't apply to them. Other players' pets can still be hit.

## The Crown of Chaos

The final crown is made in five steps:

1. **Craft the Incomplete Crown of Chaos** from all nine magical crowns, in any order (each can be its
   netherite version; unlocked by picking up a Crown of Void). It reads *"ERROR ERROR ERR"* followed by six
   scrambled, ever-changing letters in the Standard Galactic Alphabet (the enchanting table's script).
2. **Open the portal:** throw a **Music Disc 11** into a normal nether portal in the Overworld (it does nothing in the Nether, so nobody gets trapped). The disc is swallowed, the
   swirl turns into purple-and-black "null" blocks and the obsidian frame into random blocks that never
   fall or update, different every time. Walk into the null blocks to enter the Chaos Arena. Breaking a null
   block destroys the whole portal.
3. **Beat the Chaos King** (below). He drops the **Soul of Chaos**.
4. **Make the Chaos Upgrade:** a soul fire then burns on the altar. Throw a Netherite Upgrade smithing
   template into it and it becomes a Chaos Upgrade.
5. **Smith the crown** at a smithing table: Chaos Upgrade + Incomplete Crown of Chaos + Soul of Chaos.

**The Chaos Arena** (`crownofchaos:chaos_arena`) is a dark purple void with a large round floating arena, about 73 blocks across (a
glitched mix of netherrack, cobblestone, mossy cobblestone and nether bricks) and a gold-and-redstone-torch
altar. A solid wall of lava that never flows rings it and climbs out of sight, so don't back off too far; bedrock seals it on the outside, underneath and on top, so nothing gets out.
Nothing spawns there on its own and there's no treasure: only the boss, what he summons, and what he drops (his units drop experience but none of their usual items).
Once he is beaten, an unbreakable null portal opens behind where you arrived and takes you back to where you
came in; it closes again if the altar is relit. Ops can also
`/execute in crownofchaos:chaos_arena run tp @s 0 65 -32`.

**The fight** (balance isn't tuned yet): lighting the altar summons **Herobrine, The Chaos King**, a
player-style boss wearing the Crown of Chaos, in a storm of lightning. Music ("Empire" by Laudividni) plays
on repeat for everyone in the arena until he is defeated.

- He floats up, invulnerable, and calls four waves of units that attack you and your army units (and never
  each other). Per player (counted when the altar is lit, so a player dying or arriving mid-fight doesn't change later waves): 5 Strays and 5 Husks; then 1 Ravager, 2 Evokers and 3 Vindicators; then
  4 Guardians and 6 Drowned; then one Warden with 25% of its health per player, hitting 25% weaker (melee and sonic boom).
- A player who arrives after the altar was lit is counted from then on: the wave in progress gets their share of extra units (or Warden health) and later waves are sized for them too.
- The first wave comes 15 seconds after he rises, so you can ready yourself and back away from the altar;
  when a wave is cleared a banner says so and the next one follows 6 seconds later.
- When the last wave falls he draws an enchanted-looking diamond sword (for show; it adds no damage), flies down onto the altar and fights you with melee and, every 12 seconds, a
  Wither Skull, a Fire Charge or a Lightning Strike. He has a Warden's health and stays on the floor, away from the lava.
- He lets out an evil laugh, heard at full volume by everyone in the arena, wherever they stand, whenever a player dies, one of his abilities hurts someone, or a new wave starts.
- When you finally kill him he lets out a death wail, heard by everyone in the arena, a large gold **VICTORY** banner fills the screen and the victory music ("Celebration") plays once.
- If every player in the arena dies or leaves, the fight clears itself and the altar must be lit again.

**The Crown of Chaos** has netherite helmet stats, a custom 3D model, no netherite upgrade, and:

- the Crown of Nether's fire and lava immunity, lava vision and lava swimming, the Crown of Wither's Wither immunity, the Crown of Winter's
  freeze immunity and the Crown of Void's teleport when hit, but not the crowns' other powers;
- every status effect the other crowns give: Haste II, Night Vision, Hero of the Village, Water Breathing, Dolphin's
  Grace and Fire Resistance, Swiftness II on the Sun, Winter and Void crowns' blocks, and Regeneration I in water;
- all the crowns' speed features: normal grip on ice, walking on powder snow (the Crown of Winter's) and the Crown of Void's faster elytra flight;
- **4 extra hearts** (28 health);
- every hostile mob leaves you alone (not the Chaos King's legion), until you hurt one, which then fights back for 10 seconds;
- **The pain of death** (primary): fires a Wither Skull and a Fire Charge where you look and, at whatever you're
  looking at, a Lightning Strike (within 30 blocks) and an Arrow Volley (within 20), all at once, for the
  abilities' usual damage;
- **Death's door** (secondary): the Crown of Darkness's Darkness Pulse, lasting 300% longer (4 times as long);
- command over every unit type; in a Commander's Table it lists and makes all of them.

## Advancements

The mod adds its own advancement tab, **The Crown of Chaos** (with the Crown of Darkness as its icon), for getting the major items. Each is awarded when
the item first enters your inventory (the last four also need their step):

- **The Crown of Chaos:** the tab itself, opened by obtaining a Golden Crown.
- **Each of the nine magical crowns** (Under, Poseidon, Nether, Darkness, Sun, Wither, Winter, Iron, Void).
  **A Crown for Every Occasion** is for having obtained all nine, not necessarily at once.
- **The Screeching Has Ceased** (Sculk Cord) and **Ra Would Be Proud** (Ankh Charm).
- **Heavy Is the Head** (any netherite crown) and **Dressed in Netherite** (all nine).
- **Something Is Wrong** (the Incomplete Crown of Chaos), **Into the Glitch** (enter the Chaos Arena),
  **The King Falls** (defeat the Chaos King), **A Piece of Chaos** (Soul of Chaos), **Glitch in the Forge**
  (Chaos Upgrade) and **Crowned in Chaos**, the challenge for smithing the finished crown.

## The lore book

You are given **A Warning from a Former King** the first time you have a Golden Crown: a book written by
someone who wore the crowns before you. It hints, in riddles about the old Herobrine stories, at the secret steps on the way to the
Crown of Chaos (what to do with the Nether portal, the altar and the soul fire), without giving them away.
Use it to read it. Its pages are scrambled until you have earned them: each page after the first three
unlocks with the advancement for the step before the secret it hints at. Lost it? Craft another from a
**Golden Crown and a Book**.

## Factions & Units (beta)

> **Beta:** the faction system's main features are complete: factions, the faction menu,
> territory, the Commander's Table, army units you can summon and command, and war. It's
> still being tested and balanced, so details may change between versions.

Players band together into factions, each with a name and color, and claim land with faction flags.

**Faction menu:** press **J** (rebindable under Options → Controls → Key Binds → Crown of Chaos - Faction
System, with the unit keys below).

- **Without a faction:** **Create** (a unique name of up to 24 characters, emoji allowed, and one of the 16
  dye colors; no two factions share a color) or **Join** (accept or decline invitations, or ask to join).
- **In a faction:**
  - **Overview:** details. The owner can rename, recolor, toggle red alert and disband.
  - **Members:** the owner can invite online players by name, accept or decline requests, remove members
    and hand over ownership (they can't leave without doing that or disbanding).
  - **Relations:** the owner sets each other faction to **Allowed** (may enter your territory unharmed),
    **Neutral** or **Targeted** (attacked in your territory).
  - **War:** declare war on factions or players, or make peace (see **War**).
  - **Factions:** every faction, with its owner, members and flags.

Members' names show their faction as a colored `[Faction Name]` tag in chat and above their heads.

**Faction Flag:** crafted from 8 wool of any color around a Faction Coin (unlocked by picking up a Faction
Coin). It belongs to the faction of whoever crafts it and shows as a banner in that faction's color, with the
faction's name floating above it. It works like any banner: put it in a loom to add patterns and dyes (the base stays your faction's color), wash layers off in a cauldron, and the patterns stay on it when it's placed and picked up again.

- Placing it (members only) claims everything within **100 blocks**, at any height. Flags of different
  factions must be at least 200 blocks apart, so territories never overlap; your own flags can overlap.
- Only members (and creative players) can break a flag or the block it stands on or hangs from; explosions
  and pistons can't. Breaking your own flag removes the claim, and the flag drops to place again.

**Territory:** entering a faction's territory shows its name in large letters in its color, with "Faction
Territory" (or "Your Faction's Territory") underneath. Leaving shows a short note above the hotbar.
**Red alert and relations** decide when your units fight in your territory (see **Summoning units**).

### Commander's Table and units

The Commander's Table makes army units. Wear a crown, or put one in its crown slot, to see the units it commands, pick
one, and put the unit's two cost items in the cost slots (either order). Taking the unit item uses up the
costs; the crown stays. Shift-click makes as many as the costs allow. Netherite crowns command the same units
as their crown. Like a crafting table it keeps no items (closing it gives them back) and turns to face you
when placed.

The Golden Crown and Under Crown command no units. Unit items look like the mob's spawn egg and are named
after it ("Pillager Unit").

**Summoning units:** use a unit item on a block, like a spawn egg, to summon the mob as a unit of your
faction (you must be in one). Units:

- are tinted a vivid version of your faction's color and outlined in it (up to 64 blocks, only while you can
  see them), and change with the faction (white factions are only outlined);
- bring allies: the vexes an **Evoker** unit calls fight for your faction too (they can't be commanded and
  don't count toward the limit);
- never block your Riptide dash (the Crown of Poseidon's ability or a Riptide trident): you pass through your
  own and your faction's units;
- can be summoned and kept on **Peaceful** difficulty too, where vanilla would remove hostile mobs (Peaceful stops mobs attacking players, and Shulker units won't shoot there, as in vanilla);
- are limited to **40 per player** (reclaim or lose some to summon more), and can't be hurt by anyone in your faction;
- never despawn or burn in daylight, and Piglins and Hoglins don't turn into zombies outside the Nether;
- Guardians treat air as water, so they float and fight on land and follow orders out of the sea; Wardens
  stay above ground and give no Darkness;
- are **passive** toward players and other units, and never attack their own faction, except:
  - **wild hostile mobs** (zombies, skeletons, creepers...) attacking you or your faction's units, or, when
    told to **Attack**, any within 24 blocks;
  - anything **you attack** (a mob, a player or a unit that isn't Allowed, never your pets), for 10 seconds after
    your last hit, after which they go back to following, standing sentry or wandering;
  - inside your territory, players and units of **Targeted** factions, and with **red alert** on, everyone
    not **Allowed** (unfactioned players included);
  - anywhere, factions and players you're at war with (see **War**).

Players and units your units would fight (the war enemies, and the Targeted or not-Allowed ones in your
territory) are **enemy combatants**. Whatever else a unit is fighting, such as a wild mob, it drops for an
enemy combatant it can see, or for what you are attacking.

**Commanding units:** you command your faction's units whose crown you're wearing (the Crown of Iron for
Pillagers, Vindicators, Ravagers and Evokers).

- **Right-click** a unit to make it a sentry (again to free it). **Sneak + right-click** selects or
  deselects it (selected units have a white outline, for you only).
- Orders go to your **selected units**, or with none selected, to **all your units within 32 blocks**.
- **Unit Commands** key (default **V**) toggles the command menu: a slim see-through row above your hotbar and
  status bars, with a status line (and a "Sneak + Click to select one unit" hint when you have nothing to
  command). It doesn't take over the mouse, camera or movement, and stays open after an order until you press
  V again. Each order has a number key (1-8), so V then 3 orders an attack without aiming:
  - **Follow Me:** they follow you, teleporting if they fall 24 blocks behind, and still fight mobs that
    attack you or your units. They come back if they end up more than 40 blocks away.
  - **Sentry:** they guard where they stand. An enemy combatant that comes into their aggro range is chased for
    as long as it stays in your territory, however far, until it dies or leaves; wild mobs are chased at most
    16 blocks from the post. Then they walk back to the post.
  - **Hunt** (key 8): they hold a post like a sentry, but go after enemy combatants anywhere in your territory,
    even far beyond their aggro range and out of sight, until they die or leave it, then walk back to the post.
  - **Attack:** they hunt enemies within 24 blocks, anywhere: players and units of every faction you
    haven't set to Allowed. Overrides sentry and following.
  - **Free:** no orders; wander, passive except for the rules above.
  - **Reclaim:** they turn back into unit items in your inventory (not while your faction is at war).
  - **Select:** selects the unit you're looking at (hold **Sneak** for every unit of that type nearby); with
    none in your crosshair, all your units nearby.
  - **Clear Selection.**
- **Units: Follow / Stop Following** key (default **B**) toggles following. Both keys rebind with the faction
  menu key.
- If a unit dies where you can't see it, you're told where.

**War:** the faction owner declares war in the menu's **War** tab, on a faction or a player, and ends it with
**Make Peace**.

- **On a faction:** your units attack all its members and units anywhere, overriding Allowed.
- **On a player** (type their name; online, or a member of any faction, or unfactioned): your units attack
  only them and their units, not the rest of their faction.
- War is one-sided: the other side is told, and their units only fight back if they declare war too.
- While your faction is at war with anyone, its members can't reclaim units.

## Changes to vanilla

These apply to everyone in a world with the mod, not just crown wearers:

- Every Ender Dragon defeat places a Dragon Egg on the exit portal, including resummoned dragons (vanilla
  only does this the first time). No extra egg is added if one is already there.
- Wardens, Elder Guardians and desert pyramids give the crafting materials listed under **Materials**.
- Nautilus mobs drop a Nautilus Shell 50% of the time when a player kills them (vanilla: 5%). Looting adds 1% per level above the first, like vanilla.
- Explosions and pistons can't destroy faction flags or the blocks holding them up.

## Installation

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) for Minecraft 26.2.
2. Download [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) for 26.2 and put it in your
   `mods` folder.
3. Put the Crown of Chaos jar in your `mods` folder.

The mod must be installed on both the client and the server, and players can't join without it.
Minecraft 26.2 needs Java 25 (the server needs it too). On a server, use the Fabric API version built
for 26.2 (0.161.0 or newer).

**Compatibility:** tested in a large Fabric modpack (about 85 mods, including Create, Sodium,
Iris, Entity Culling, Lithium, ImmediatelyFast and Better Combat). Ore Sense's outlines are
drawn in a way that also works with Create, whose renderer hides the glowing outline of
display blocks. With Better Combat (or other mods that show your body in first person), your
own crown is hidden from your first-person view so it doesn't cover the screen; it still shows
in third person, in the inventory and to other players.
